Week 15 • Dog Breed

🐕 Shih Tzu – Beautiful Long Coat, Needs Daily Grooming and Eye Care

The Shih Tzu is a small, friendly dog with a long, flowing coat. They were bred to be companion dogs for Chinese royalty. They are loving, playful, and good with families. At Dr. Deepa pet vet clinic, we see many Shih Tzus because they are very popular in India. But they need a lot of grooming – daily brushing to prevent painful mats. Their flat face also causes breathing problems, and their large eyes can get injured easily.

🐾 Key things to remember: Shih Tzus need daily brushing – if you skip even a few days, mats form. Their eyes need daily cleaning to prevent staining and infections. They cannot tolerate heat – keep them cool.

What does a Shih Tzu look like?

  • Size: Small – 4–7 kg. Height 20–28 cm.
  • Coat: Long, thick, double coat – comes in many colours. Grows continuously, like human hair.
  • Build: Sturdy, compact, with a short muzzle (flat face), large round eyes, and floppy ears.

Why Shih Tzus are popular in India

  • Affectionate: They love to cuddle and be lap dogs – great for apartments.
  • Good with children: They are patient and gentle.
  • Low exercise needs: Short walks and indoor play are enough – good for small homes.
  • Alert: They bark at strangers – good watchdogs.
✂️ Dr. Deepa’s warning: “Shih Tzus need daily brushing. Their coat mats easily, and mats pull the skin, causing pain and infection. If you cannot brush daily, keep their coat trimmed short – a ‘puppy cut’ is much easier.”

Common health problems in Shih Tzus

  • Eye problems: Large, prominent eyes are prone to dry eye, corneal ulcers, and scratches. Clean around eyes daily.
  • Brachycephalic airway syndrome: Flat face causes snoring, wheezing, and heat intolerance. Avoid hot weather.
  • Dental disease: Crowded teeth trap food. Brush teeth weekly and get yearly dental cleanings.
  • Patellar luxation: Loose kneecaps – can cause limping. Keep them lean.
  • Ear infections: Floppy ears trap moisture. Clean ears weekly.

Grooming – high maintenance

  • Brush daily: Use a slicker brush and steel comb. Work through the coat in sections. Pay special attention behind ears, armpits, and belly – mats form there first.
  • Bathe every 2–3 weeks: Use a good dog shampoo and conditioner. Dry thoroughly – damp fur mats faster.
  • Eye cleaning – daily: Wipe the area under eyes with a soft, damp cloth to remove tears and prevent staining.
  • Ear cleaning – weekly: Use a cotton ball – never Q‑tips.
  • Trim nails every 3–4 weeks. Brush teeth weekly.
  • Professional grooming every 4–6 weeks: Most owners keep the coat trimmed short for easier maintenance.

Exercise and environment

Shih Tzus are low‑energy dogs, but they still need daily walks.

  • Walks: 15–20 minutes twice a day. Avoid hot sun – use the paw test (Week 13).
  • Indoor play: Fetch, tug toys, puzzle feeders – they enjoy simple games.
  • Heat sensitivity: They overheat easily. Keep them in air conditioning or cool rooms in summer.

Training and behaviour

  • Shih Tzus are smart but can be stubborn. Use positive reinforcement (treats, praise).
  • House‑training can be difficult – consistency and patience are needed.
  • They can develop separation anxiety if left alone for long hours – they want to be with their family.

Feeding guidelines

  • High‑quality small breed dog food – they need good nutrition for their coat.
  • For an adult Shih Tzu, 1/2 to 3/4 cup of dry food per day (divided into two meals). Adjust based on activity.
  • Do not overfeed – obesity worsens breathing and joint problems.
  • Always fresh, clean water.

Real story from our clinic

A Shih Tzu named “Lucky” came in with severe matting. His coat was so tangled that we had to shave him completely. Under the mats, his skin was red, infected, and painful. The owner had not brushed him for weeks. We treated the skin infection and showed the owner how to brush daily. Now Lucky gets a short “puppy cut” every 6 weeks – much easier for the owner. The owner said, “I didn’t know mats hurt them. Now I keep his coat short and brush him every day.”

Should you get a Shih Tzu?

Yes, if you:

  • Have time for daily brushing or budget for regular professional grooming.
  • Can clean their eyes and ears weekly.
  • Live in a cool home or have air conditioning in summer.
  • Want a small, affectionate, indoor companion dog.

Maybe not, if you:

  • Cannot commit to daily brushing – their coat will mat painfully.
  • Live in a very hot, humid city without AC.
  • Want a low‑grooming, independent dog (get an Indie dog instead).
  • Are not prepared for potential eye or breathing problems.

Action for Week 15

  1. If you have a Shih Tzu, check their coat today – run a comb through. Any mats? If yes, gently brush them out or have a groomer shave them.
  2. Clean their eyes daily – use a soft, damp cloth to wipe away tears.
  3. Consider a short “puppy cut” – easier for both of you.
  4. If you are thinking of getting a Shih Tzu, adopt from a rescue – many need homes due to owners not handling the grooming.
